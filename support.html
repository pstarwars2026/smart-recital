Support

Let’s get the music playing.

Smart Recital is in development and is not yet available on the App Store. For product questions, bug reports, accessibility requests or privacy concerns, contact the developer through GitHub Issues.

Contact support on GitHub

A GitHub account is required to post. Issues are public: never include private pairing links, passwords, receipts, Apple Account information or copyrighted music files.

Piano connection

Use a MIDI-capable keyboard or player-piano system and an appropriate USB/MIDI connection. Turn the instrument on, connect it, then choose it from the cable menu in Smart Recital. Choose a piece and select Play. A standard acoustic piano without MIDI hardware cannot receive playback.

Phone companion

The host iPad or Mac requires Pro. Put the phone and host on the same trusted Wi-Fi, allow Local Network access, then enable Phone companion and scan its QR code. Keep the host app running. Guest links allow requests only; the host approves additions to the queue. Turning the companion off expires both links. Public or guest Wi-Fi can block devices from connecting to each other.

Purchases and restore

Pro is planned as a one-time upgrade, not a subscription. In the released app, use Pro & purchases → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchase and restore testing is still in progress; no release date is promised.

What helps us investigate

Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, piano model, connection type and the steps that led to the problem. Remove identifying details from screenshots before posting.
