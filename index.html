See the music before you upgrade.

Free includes a replayable 20-second falling-note preview for any song. It starts with your first playback. When the preview ends, the complete song keeps playing on your piano. Pro keeps the performance view open for the whole piece.

Your phone, the remote.

Pro lets you line up music from your phone on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Scan the host’s QR code to control playback, or share a guest link for requests you approve. Keep Smart Recital running on the host iPad or Mac.

The local companion uses HTTP. Treat control links as private and use a network you trust.

Five original demos to get started.

These pieces were composed for Smart Recital and are included in both Free and Pro. Import your own MIDI collection to make the app yours.

Before you connect

A compatible MIDI keyboard or player-piano system is required. Sending MIDI to a digital piano produces sound; moving acoustic piano keys requires a MIDI-capable player piano. Minimum planned versions: iPadOS 26 and macOS 26.
