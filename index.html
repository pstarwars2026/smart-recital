Your phone, the remote.

Pro lets you line up music from your phone on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Scan the host’s QR code to control playback, or share a separate guest link for requests you approve. Keep Smart Recital running on the host iPad or Mac.

The local companion uses HTTP, not encrypted HTTPS. Treat control links as private and use a network you trust.

A collection with a source for every piece.

Classical favorites, romantic piano and ragtime, using credited public-domain editions from the Mutopia Project. Complete movements are labeled as movements. Smart Recital does not claim these editions as original arrangements.

The full collection is listed in the app under Collection & music credits.

Before you connect

A compatible MIDI keyboard or player-piano system is required. Sending MIDI to a digital piano produces sound; moving acoustic piano keys requires a MIDI-capable player piano. Minimum planned versions: iPadOS 26 and macOS 26.
