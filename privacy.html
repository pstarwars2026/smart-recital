Privacy · Updated September 22, 2026

Your library stays with you.

This policy covers Smart Recital’s iPad and Mac apps, local phone companion and this product website. The app is currently in development.

No app account or cloud music upload

Smart Recital does not require an app account. The developer does not operate a cloud service for your piano performances or music library. No advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK is included. The app does not send music to an AI service.

Local music and settings

Imported MIDI files, saved playlists and queue information are stored in the app’s local storage. The app reads MIDI notes and device information to connect to compatible instruments and display performances. It does not record microphone audio. You can remove imported music and playlists in the app. Operating-system backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

Phone companion

When you enable the Pro companion, Smart Recital starts a local server on the host device. A paired phone can access song titles, composer names, queue information and playback state on the same Wi-Fi. This may include names of imported music and the connected piano. A private control link permits playback and queue commands; a separate guest link permits song requests.

The companion uses unencrypted HTTP on the local network. Use trusted Wi-Fi and do not post pairing links publicly. Links expire when the companion is turned off or its session ends. Pairing access is kept in the phone tab’s session storage so refreshing the page does not require scanning again. The developer does not receive companion traffic.

Apple purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases. Smart Recital uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify entitlements and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles App Store account, payment and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy and your device settings.

Website and support

This static website has no added analytics, advertising or contact form. GitHub hosts the site and support issues and may process technical data under its own privacy policy. Information you submit to GitHub Issues is public. Do not include sensitive information in a support request. We use the information you voluntarily post to respond to the issue.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues without posting private data. This page will be updated if the app’s handling of information changes.
